How Let Us Care works

1

Tap “I need care”

On the first screen, tap the blue button.

2

Choose the care

Toddler, Elder, Patient, Mother and newborn, Special needs or Physiotherapy.

3

See carers near you

Nearest first. “✓ Number verified” means we checked their mobile number.

4

Call or WhatsApp

Talk about the work, timings and pay. You can contact up to 10 carers a day.

5

Meet and check

Before hiring, see the carer's original Aadhaar card and police verification certificate.

6

Make a care sheet

Tap “Make care sheet”. Write food, medicine, timings and emergency numbers. Send it to the carer on WhatsApp, share it as a picture or print it.

Good to know

  • The app is free. No commission. You pay the carer directly.
  • Your care sheet stays only on your phone.
  • The carer can tap Listen to hear the care sheet in their language.
  • Change the language any time at the top.
I need care
1

Tap “I want to give care”

On the first screen, tap the orange button.

2

Fill the short form

Your name, mobile number two times, female or male, the care you give. Tap “Use my current location”. Photo is optional. Tap Register.

3

Verify on WhatsApp

Tap the green button and press Send. Do not change the message.

4

Wait for approval

We check your number and approve you. Then families near you can see you and call you.

5

Complete your profile

Add skills, certificate, rate and working time. More details bring more calls.

6

Busy or free?

Busy with a family? Turn “Available for work” off. Free again? Turn it on.

7

Share your profile

Tap “Share my profile” to send your details to families on WhatsApp.

Your promise

  • Follow the family's care sheet.
  • Do not give any medicine unless the family tells you.
  • If you see a danger sign, call the family at once, and 108 if needed.
  • Carry your original ID card. The family pays you directly. The app takes nothing.
