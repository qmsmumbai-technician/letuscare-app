Delete your Let Us Care account

Let Us Care – Carers Near You · by Abode Digital Technology

Families do not have an account. Everything a family types in the app stays only on their phone. To remove it, clear the app's data in phone settings, or uninstall the app.

Carers can ask us to delete their carer profile at any time, using the steps below.

How a carer can delete their account

  1. Send us a WhatsApp message or an email from your registered mobile number (or mention it in the email).
  2. Write: "Please delete my Let Us Care profile", with your name and registered mobile number.
  3. We confirm it is your number and delete your profile, normally within 7 days. We reply when it is done.

Request deletion on WhatsApp Request by email

Want to stop getting calls without deleting? Open the app and turn "Available for work" off. Families will not see you until you turn it on again.

Delete only some of your data

  • Yourself, in the app: open Let Us Care → "I want to give care" → Complete my profile. Clear or change your skills, rate, references, emergency contact, note about yourself and other details, or change your photo, and tap Save changes. The change is immediate.
  • Ask us: send a WhatsApp message or email (buttons above) saying which details to remove, for example "remove my photo". We do it within 7 days.

What is deleted

  • Your carer profile: name, mobile number, gender, age, photo, care services, skills, certificates, experience, working times, rate, languages, area and location, references, emergency contact and the note about yourself.
  • Your approval, availability and last-seen details.

What is kept

  • Nothing from your profile is kept after deletion.
  • WhatsApp messages or emails you sent us stay in our WhatsApp or email inbox, as with any message. Backup copies are removed within 30 days.
